The Model

How We Build

A repeatable, proven process from raw land to sold home — executed in 100 days.

01
Acquire Raw Land
We identify and acquire 1-acre parcels in McDowell County, NC — land with strong perc tests, road access, and proximity to growing communities.
02
Horizontal Construction
Site prep, septic installation, well drilling, grading, and footers — all coordinated and executed in the first 30 days after closing.
03
Deliver & Build Out
New construction manufactured home delivered and set on permanent brick foundation. All trades — electrical, plumbing, HVAC — completed in sequence.
04
List & Sell
Final inspections, CO obtained, professional photos, and listed at market. No comparable new construction has sold under breakeven in McDowell County in over 3 years.
